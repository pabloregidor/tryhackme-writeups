## Key concepts

- Threat modelling is a proccedure that has the nexte steps: Identifying the critical points, analysing its vulnerabilities, creating a plan to secure them and prevent future vulnerabilities(It can be based in various frameworks)
- Unified Kill Chain is more detailed than others(18 phases and it covers the posibility of an attacker going back an forth) and more modern. 
- In the initial goals for the foothold, the analysis of the defence evaluation, is where the most valuable information is hide for the security team.
- Once the adversary has the foothold goal, they begin the through goal, that is netowork propagation, triying to escalate privileges, steal credentials and other data.
- In the last phase, the attacker would seek for information, steal the valuable one and encrypt, delete or whatever action with the local data that would make him achieve his objective. If the  objective is monetary, he would encrypt the data and ask for a payment to release it.
